#  Global Video Games Sales Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive and comprehensive data visualization project built using **Tableau**, analyzing **11,493 games, 579 publishers, and 31 platforms** to uncover global sales trends, regional preferences, and hardware evolution over the years.

---

##  Project Overview
This project transforms massive gaming industry datasets into clear, actionable visual insights. It explores how video game sales differ drastically across global markets (NA, EU, JP), highlights top-performing franchises and publishers, and tracks the golden era of hardware adoption.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Findings

### 🌍 Regional Preferences
- **North America & Europe:** Action, Sports, and Shooter dominate sales, driven by blockbuster franchises like *Grand Theft Auto* and *Call of Duty*, alongside legendary platforms like the Wii, PS3, and Xbox 360.
- **Japan:** Features a completely unique market dynamic where **Role-Playing (RPG)** leads with over **350M+ sales**, heavily anchored by iconic franchises like *Pokémon* and *Animal Crossing* and handheld giants like the Nintendo DS.

### 📈 Industry Evolution
- Global video game sales peaked around **2008–2010**, propelled by a massive wave of mainstream hardware adoption led by the Nintendo Wii, PS3, and Xbox 360.

### 🏢 Top Industry Players
- **Nintendo** stands out as the ultimate powerhouse in the dataset, fueled by massive system-sellers like *Wii Sports*, alongside industry giants like Electronic Arts, Activision, and Sony Computer Entertainment.

---

##  Technical Implementation

### 1. Data Exploration & Processing
- Cleaned and structured multi-dimensional gaming data covering over 11,000 titles.
- Handled regional sales metrics (NA, EU, JP, Other) for comparative cross-market analysis.

### 2. Tableau Visualization & Features
- **Multi-Dimensional Filtering:** Dynamic controls allowing users to filter by platform, genre, publisher, and release year.
- **Geographic & Regional Comparison:** Side-by-side breakdowns highlighting distinct gaming habits across international markets.
- **Time-Series Charts:** Tracking yearly global sales trends and industry turning points.
- **Ranked Bar Charts & Metrics:** Showcasing top publishers and best-selling game titles.

### 3. UI/UX Design
- Clean, professional dashboard layout optimized for readability, user engagement, and intuitive data storytelling.
